Key VR features

Immersive visuals

Head-mounted displays present a virtual scene across the user's field of view.

Motion tracking

Sensors track head and body movement so the virtual view can respond naturally.

Spatial audio

Directional sound helps users understand where sounds are coming from.

Real-time interaction

Controllers and tracking allow users to interact with objects in a virtual space.
